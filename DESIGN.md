# EiFlow design direction

## Thesis
EiFlow is a signal garden for attention: a cool mineral workspace where apricot signals the next action, mint confirms a return, and periwinkle maps time and reflection. It refuses both the gray enterprise console and the soft wellness dashboard.

## World
The replacement visual world is cool mist canvas, opaque mineral panels, deep pine navigation, graphite rules, and purposeful signal colors. The product feels like a calm instrument rather than a board of cards.

## Tokens
- Canvas: `#EEF2F5`; surface: `#FBFCFD`; raised surface: `#FFFFFF`.
- Ink: `#17242B`; muted: `#64747D`; pine: `#1E4842`.
- Apricot action: `#F4B36B`; mint completion: `#7AD9BC`; periwinkle planning: `#A7B6F5`; coral exception: `#E98272`.
- Radii: 8px controls, 12px compact items, 18px primary panels, 24px feature moments.
- Shadows: offset, soft graphite depth; no decorative blur halos.

## Composition
Operate mode. Every page has one lead decision and one supporting rail. Use signal lines, dot markers, and compact traces to connect action to rhythm. The mobile shell uses five high-frequency destinations in a bottom navigation.

## Type and motion
Use a rounded display stack (`Arial Rounded MT Bold`, `Trebuchet MS`, `ui-rounded`) paired with a clean system UI stack. Keep copy readable, labels sparse, and the primary action obvious. Use a 320ms page reveal, 160–200ms control feedback, and 420ms progress transitions. Honor reduced motion.

## Accessibility
Body text maintains 4.5:1 contrast; status is never color-only; all icon buttons are labelled; controls are keyboard reachable with visible focus rings; dialogs have labelled headings and `aria-modal`; touch targets are at least 44px.
